Pause so the user can review the change you just made, then continue with their decision.

OpenCode computes the exact diff since the previous checkpoint and shows it to the user next to your explanation. The user then approves the step, asks for a revision, or stops the run.

When to call it:
- After each meaningful change: one logical unit of work, such as adding a function, updating its callers, or adding a test.
- On its own, after your edits have finished. Never call it in the same response as an edit.
- Only when you changed files since the last checkpoint.

Parameters:
- title: a short name for the step (3-8 words)
- why: one or two sentences on why you made this change
- impact: one or two sentences on what this change affects, such as callers, behavior, or tests

The result tells you what the user decided:
- Approved: continue with the next step.
- Revision requested: apply the user's feedback to the same step, then call checkpoint again before starting anything new.
